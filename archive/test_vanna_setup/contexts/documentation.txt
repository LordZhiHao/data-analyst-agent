# Membership & Groceries User Profile Dataset

This dataset contains information about grocery store customers, their membership details, and shopping behaviors.

## Table Structure: 'sqlAgentTestv1.groceries'

### Customer Identifiers
- id: Unique identifier for each customer (primary key)
- gender: Customer's gender identifier (M/F/Other)
- shared_account: Boolean flag (1=account shared with family members, 0=single user)

### Membership Information
- membership_tier: Membership level (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum)
- membership_auto_renew: Boolean flag (1=auto-renewal enabled, 0=manual renewal)
- membership_fee: Annual fee paid for membership in dollars
- membership_start_date: Date when the customer's membership began (YYYY-MM-DD)

### Digital Engagement
- push_notification_enabled: Boolean flag (1=notifications enabled, 0=disabled)
- have_app: Boolean flag (1=has installed our app, 0=does not have app)
- app_engagement_score: Numerical score from 0-100 measuring app activity level

### Shopping Behavior
- bought_store_brand: Boolean flag (1=purchases store brand products, 0=only buys name brands)
- promotion_participation_count: Number of promotions customer has participated in
- average_basket_size: Average dollar amount spent per shopping trip
- use_count: Number of store visits in the last 12 months
- reward_points_used: Number of reward points redeemed for discounts/products

## Business Context

This dataset is used for several analytical purposes:
1. Customer segmentation for targeted marketing
2. Membership tier optimization
3. App feature and notification strategy
4. Store brand adoption analysis
5. Promotion effectiveness measurement

Higher membership tiers pay larger fees but receive better discounts and exclusive offers. Our goal is to increase membership tier, app engagement, and store brand adoption across all customer segments.
